# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free environment for everyone. This Code of Conduct applies to all spaces managed by the Joseph R Lane Center Of Leadership community, including GitHub, forums, social media, events, and direct communications.

---

## Our Standards

### Examples of Behavior That Create a Positive Environment

- ✅ Using welcoming and inclusive language
- ✅ Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- ✅ Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- ✅ Focusing on what is best for the community
- ✅ Showing empathy towards other community members
- ✅ Acknowledging the value of different perspectives
- ✅ Supporting newer members and learners
- ✅ Celebrating others' achievements

### Examples of Unacceptable Behavior

- ❌ Harassment or discrimination based on protected characteristics
- ❌ Violent threats or violent language directed against another person
- ❌ Sexist, racist, or otherwise discriminatory jokes and language
- ❌ Posting or threatening to post others' private information
- ❌ Personal insults, particularly those using racist or sexist terms
- ❌ Unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- ❌ Advocating for, or encouraging, any of the above behaviors
- ❌ Continued harassment after being asked to stop
- ❌ Deliberate intimidation
- ❌ Stalking or following

## Enforcement

### Who This Applies To
- All members of the community
- Contributors and maintainers
- Event attendees
- Content creators
- Moderators

### Reporting Violations

**If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior:**

1. **Document** - Note what happened, when, who was involved
2. **Report** - Send to conduct@josephrlanecenter.com
3. **Include Context** - URLs, screenshots, participants, exact language
4. **Confidentiality** - Your identity will be protected

**Report Information:**
- What happened?
- Who was involved?
- When did it occur?
- Where did it occur?
- Why do you believe it violated the Code of Conduct?

### Response Process

**Investigation:**
- Acknowledgment within 24 hours
- Confidential investigation begins
- All parties given opportunity to respond
- Takes 3-7 business days typically

**Outcomes:**
- **No violation** - Investigation concluded
- **Warning** - Private warning to member
- **Temporary suspension** - 1-30 day removal
- **Permanent removal** - Complete community removal
- **Legal referral** - For serious violations

**Communication:**
- All parties notified of decision
- Reasoning provided (where appropriate)
- Appeal process available (30 days)
- Action taken (violation corrected or removed)

### Appeal Process

**If you disagree with a decision:**

1. **Submit Appeal** - Within 30 days to appeals@josephrlanecenter.com
2. **Provide Reasoning** - Why do you disagree?
3. **New Review** - Independent reviewer examines case
4. **Decision** - Appeal upheld, overturned, or modified
5. **Final** - Decision is final

## Accountability

### For Moderators
- Follow same standards as community
- Recuse themselves from cases involving friends/family
- Maintain confidentiality
- Act impartially and fairly
- Explain decisions clearly
- Allow appeals

### For Maintainers
- Model inclusive, professional behavior
- Respond promptly to reports
- Take all violations seriously
- Create safe environment for contributors
- Lead by example

### For Organizers
- Enforce Code of Conduct at events
- Provide safe space to report violations
- Have incident response plan
- Follow up with attendees if needed
- Make accessibility accommodations

## Special Situations

### Discrimination & Harassment

**Definition:** Unwelcome conduct based on protected characteristics (race, color, religion, gender, national origin, age, disability, sexual orientation, gender identity, etc.)

**Response:**
- Immediate investigation
- Zero tolerance
- Faster resolution timeline
- Victim support offered
- Possible legal referral

### Sexual Harassment

**Definition:** Unwelcome sexual advances, requests for sexual favors, or other verbal/physical conduct of a sexual nature

**Response:**
- Immediate investigation
- Safety measures implemented
- Counseling resources provided
- Legal referral if needed
- Community notification (if appropriate)

### Threats & Violence

**Definition:** Any threats of violence or violent language

**Response:**
- Immediate removal
- Safety measures taken
- Law enforcement contacted (if needed)
- Legal action pursued
- Community notified (if necessary)

## FAQs

### Q: What if I accidentally say something offensive?
**A:** We all make mistakes. If corrected, apologize, listen, and do better. Growth is part of our community.

### Q: Is this just about discrimination?
**A:** No. The Code of Conduct covers harassment, threats, doxxing, sexual misconduct, and other violations.

### Q: What if someone reports me unfairly?
**A:** We investigate all reports carefully. You'll have a chance to respond. The appeal process protects fairness.

### Q: Will my report be confidential?
**A:** Yes. Your identity and details are kept confidential within the investigation team. We only share what's necessary.

### Q: What if the violation happened on another platform?
**A:** If it impacts our community, we may address it. Context matters (severity, frequency, connection to community).

### Q: Can I be removed for one mistake?
**A:** Usually not. We use escalating responses. Severe violations (threats, harassment) may result in immediate removal.

### Q: What if I disagree with the decision?
**A:** You can appeal within 30 days to appeals@josephrlanecenter.com. An independent reviewer will re-examine.

## Community Health & Safety

### Creating Psychological Safety

**Leaders:**
- Encourage questions and dissenting opinions
- Acknowledge and learn from mistakes
- Create space for everyone to contribute
- Show vulnerability and authenticity

**Members:**
- Assume good intent
- Ask clarifying questions before judging
- Support peers who make mistakes
- Celebrate diverse perspectives

### Preventing Burnout

**Recognition:**
- Acknowledge contributions
- Celebrate milestones
- Share appreciation publicly
- Provide constructive, not just critical, feedback

**Balance:**
- Respect work-life balance
- No expectation of 24/7 availability
- Flexible participation options
- Mental health support available
