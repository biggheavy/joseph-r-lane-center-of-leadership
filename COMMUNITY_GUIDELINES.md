# Community Guidelines

## Welcome to the Joseph R Lane Center Of Leadership Community!

We're building a global community dedicated to leadership development, continuous learning, and mutual growth. These guidelines help us maintain a respectful, inclusive, and productive environment for everyone.

---

## Our Community Values

### 🤝 **Respect**
Treat all community members with dignity and respect. We celebrate diversity of backgrounds, experiences, and perspectives.

### 📚 **Learning**
We're committed to continuous growth. We learn from each other, ask questions, and share knowledge openly.

### 🌍 **Inclusivity**
Everyone has a voice. We welcome people from all backgrounds, cultures, industries, and experience levels.

### 🎯 **Excellence**
We strive for quality in our work and interactions. High standards in both content and conduct.

### 🤲 **Service**
Leadership is about service. We support each other in achieving our goals and serving our organizations and communities.

---

## Code of Conduct

### ✅ DO

- **Be Respectful** - Treat others how you'd like to be treated
- **Listen Actively** - Understand others' perspectives before responding
- **Contribute Positively** - Share knowledge, help others learn
- **Ask Questions** - Curiosity drives growth
- **Give Credit** - Acknowledge others' ideas and contributions
- **Provide Constructive Feedback** - Help each other improve
- **Respect Confidentiality** - Don't share others' personal information
- **Follow Platform Rules** - Adhere to terms of service

### ❌ DON'T

- **Harassment** - No personal attacks, bullying, or discrimination
- **Hate Speech** - No racist, sexist, or discriminatory language
- **Spam** - No excessive self-promotion or unwanted advertisements
- **Misinformation** - Don't spread false or misleading information
- **Copyright Violation** - Respect intellectual property rights
- **Trolling** - Don't deliberately provoke or upset others
- **Doxxing** - Don't share others' personal information
- **Illegal Activity** - No content violating laws or regulations

## Community Spaces & Guidelines

### 1. Discussion Forums

**Purpose:** Share experiences, ask questions, help peers

**Guidelines:**
- Stay on topic
- Search before posting duplicates
- Use clear, descriptive titles
- Include relevant context
- Mark questions as solved when answered
- Don't post sensitive information

**Example Posts:**
```
✅ GOOD: "Struggling with delegation - how do you balance 
          micromanagement with giving autonomy?"

❌ BAD: "Delegation sucks"
```

### 2. Study Groups

**Purpose:** Collaborate with peers on courses and projects

**Guidelines:**
- Establish group norms together
- Respect different schedules and timezones
- Share resources and knowledge
- Support struggling members
- Celebrate achievements together
- Keep group size manageable (8-15 people)

**Recommended Activities:**
- Weekly discussion calls
- Shared study schedules
- Project collaboration
- Peer review of assignments
- Accountability partnerships

### 3. Networking Events

**Purpose:** Build professional connections

**Guidelines:**
- Be authentic and genuine
- Listen more than you speak
- Follow up respectfully
- Exchange contact info appropriately
- Respect boundaries
- Don't make others uncomfortable

**Do's:**
- Introduce yourself genuinely
- Ask meaningful questions
- Share your experience and goals
- Offer help to others
- Keep business cards handy
- Follow up within a week

**Don'ts:**
- Don't pressure for sales
- Don't dominate conversations
- Don't share without permission
- Don't overlap others' time
- Don't make assumptions

### 4. Mentorship Program

**Purpose:** Connect experienced leaders with learners

**Mentor Responsibilities:**
- Commit to regular meetings
- Provide honest feedback
- Share relevant experiences
- Challenge your mentee constructively
- Respect confidentiality
- Be responsive

**Mentee Responsibilities:**
- Show initiative and preparedness
- Come with specific questions
- Act on feedback
- Respect your mentor's time
- Report progress
- Express appreciation

**Best Practices:**
- Set clear expectations upfront
- Schedule regular (bi-weekly or monthly) meetings
- Establish mutual goals
- Provide progress updates
- Adjust approach as needed

### 5. Content Sharing

**Purpose:** Share learning resources and insights

**Guidelines:**

**What's Welcome:**
- Course recommendations
- Articles and research
- Case studies
- Personal learning experiences
- Tools and templates
- Success stories

**Before Sharing:**
- Verify the source is credible
- Check for copyright/licensing
- Add your perspective or summary
- Provide context for why it matters
- Ask permission if personal stories

**Avoid Sharing:**
- Proprietary company information
- Personal financial details
- Unlicensed copyrighted material
- Unverified claims
- Excessive self-promotion

**Format Tips:**
```
Title: [Meaningful title]
Source: [Link + Author]
Why Share: [Brief explanation]
Key Takeaway: [Main insight]
Discussion: [Question to spark discussion]
```

## Reporting & Moderation

### Reporting Issues

**How to Report:**
1. **In-Platform:** Use "Report" button on content
2. **Email:** community@josephrlanecenter.com
3. **Moderation Form:** Fill out report with context
4. **Urgent:** Contact support@josephrlanecenter.com

**What to Include:**
- Specific content/user involved
- Description of violation
- Screenshots or links
- Your contact information
- When it occurred

**Your Reports Are:**
- ✅ Handled confidentially
- ✅ Investigated promptly
- ✅ Protected from retaliation
- ✅ Given follow-up updates

### Moderation Process

**Warnings (First Offense)**
- Violation explained
- Content removed or edited
- Education provided
- Second chance offered

**Suspension (Repeated Violations)**
- Temporary access removal (1-30 days)
- Reason clearly communicated
- Path to reinstatement provided
- Appeal process available

**Removal (Severe/Repeated)**
- Permanent removal from community
- Content archived for safety
- Appeal available for consideration

**Transparent Moderation:**
- We explain our actions
- We provide recourse
- We review decisions carefully
- We publish moderation principles

## Privacy & Confidentiality

### Protecting Your Information

**What We Protect:**
- Personal information (never shared without consent)
- Contact details (not sold or spammed)
- Learning progress (private by default)
- Career history (your control who sees it)
- Conversations (encrypted in private chats)

**What You Share:**
- Public posts are visible to community

**Confidentiality Agreement:**

- Don't record sessions without permission

- 📋 **GDPR compliant**

## Conflict Resolution

### When Disagreements Happen

**Step 1: Direct Communication**
- Message the person privately
- Explain your concern respectfully
- Listen to their perspective
- Try to find common ground

**Step 2: Mediation**
- Contact a community moderator
- Both parties share perspective
- Moderator helps find solution
- Document the resolution

**Step 3: Escalation**
- If unresolved, contact community team
- Formal investigation conducted
- Written decision provided
- Appeal process available

**Tips for Healthy Conflict:**
- Focus on behavior, not character
- Use "I" statements ("I felt...")
- Listen without interrupting
- Seek to understand, not win
- Apologize sincerely if wrong
- Move forward together

## FAQs

### Q: What if I see inappropriate content?
**A:** Use the Report button, email community@josephrlanecenter.com, or contact a moderator. We respond within 24 hours.

### Q: Can I share my company's products?
**A:** Yes, if it's relevant and honest. Avoid excessive self-promotion. Follow "5/95 rule" - 5% self-promotion, 95% value.

### Q: How do I find study groups?
**A:** Visit the Study Groups section, filter by course/topic, or create your own group.

### Q: What if I disagree with a moderation decision?
**A:** Submit an appeal to the moderation team within 7 days with your reasoning. We'll review and respond.

### Q: Can I share content from other platforms?
**A:** Yes, if you have permission. Include the source and original author. Don't violate copyright.

### Q: How do I report spam or harassment?
**A:** Use the Report feature, include relevant details and context, and we'll investigate immediately.

### Q: Is there support for non-English speakers?
**A:** We're working on multilingual support. Contact us about your language needs.

### Q: Can I delete my account?
**A:** Yes. Go to Settings > Privacy > Delete Account. Your data will be removed within 30 days.
