# Community Guidelines

## Welcome to the Joseph R Lane Center Of Leadership Community!

We're building a global community dedicated to leadership development, continuous learning, and mutual growth. These guidelines help us maintain a respectful, inclusive, and productive environment for everyone.

---

## Our Community Values

### 🤝 **Respect**
Treat all community members with dignity and respect. We celebrate diversity of backgrounds, experiences, and perspectives.

### 📚 **Learning**
We're committed to continuous growth. We learn from each other, ask questions, and share knowledge openly.

### 🌍 **Inclusivity**
Everyone has a voice. We welcome people from all backgrounds, cultures, industries, and experience levels.

### 🎯 **Excellence**
We strive for quality in our work and interactions. High standards in both content and conduct.

### 🤲 **Service**
Leadership is about service. We support each other in achieving our goals and serving our organizations and communities.

---

## Code of Conduct

### ✅ DO

- **Be Respectful** - Treat others how you'd like to be treated
- **Listen Actively** - Understand others' perspectives before responding
- **Contribute Positively** - Share knowledge, help others learn
- **Ask Questions** - Curiosity drives growth
- **Give Credit** - Acknowledge others' ideas and contributions
- **Provide Constructive Feedback** - Help each other improve
- **Respect Confidentiality** - Don't share others' personal information
- **Follow Platform Rules** - Adhere to terms of service

### ❌ DON'T

- **Harassment** - No personal attacks, bullying, or discrimination
- **Hate Speech** - No racist, sexist, or discriminatory language
- **Spam** - No excessive self-promotion or unwanted advertisements
- **Misinformation** - Don't spread false or misleading information
- **Copyright Violation** - Respect intellectual property rights
- **Trolling** - Don't deliberately provoke or upset others
- **Doxxing** - Don't share others' personal information
- **Illegal Activity** - No content violating laws or regulations

## Community Spaces & Guidelines

### 1. Discussion Forums

**Purpose:** Share experiences, ask questions, help peers

**Guidelines:**
- Stay on topic
- Search before posting duplicates
- Use clear, descriptive titles
- Include relevant context
- Mark questions as solved when answered
- Don't post sensitive information

### 2. Study Groups

**Purpose:** Collaborate with peers on courses and projects

**Guidelines:**
- Establish group norms together
- Respect different schedules and timezones
- Share resources and knowledge
- Support struggling members
- Celebrate achievements together
- Keep group size manageable (8-15 people)

### 3. Networking Events

**Purpose:** Build professional connections

**Guidelines:**
- Be authentic and genuine
- Listen more than you speak
- Follow up respectfully
- Exchange contact info appropriately
- Respect boundaries
- Don't make others uncomfortable

### 4. Mentorship Program

**Purpose:** Connect experienced leaders with learners

**Mentor Responsibilities:**
- Commit to regular meetings
- Provide honest feedback
- Share relevant experiences
- Challenge your mentee constructively
- Respect confidentiality
- Be responsive

**Mentee Responsibilities:**
- Show initiative and preparedness
- Come with specific questions
- Act on feedback
- Respect your mentor's time
- Report progress
- Express appreciation

## Reporting & Moderation

### Reporting Issues

**How to Report:**
1. **In-Platform:** Use "Report" button on content
2. **Email:** community@josephrlanecenter.com
3. **Moderation Form:** Fill out report with context
4. **Urgent:** Contact support@josephrlanecenter.com

### Moderation Process

**Warnings (First Offense)**
- Violation explained
- Content removed or edited
- Education provided
- Second chance offered

**Suspension (Repeated Violations)**
- Temporary access removal (1-30 days)
- Reason clearly communicated
- Path to reinstatement provided
- Appeal process available

**Removal (Severe/Repeated)**
- Permanent removal from community
- Content archived for safety
- Appeal available for consideration

## Privacy & Confidentiality

### Protecting Your Information

**What We Protect:**
- Personal information (never shared without consent)
- Contact details (not sold or spammed)
- Learning progress (private by default)
- Career history (your control who sees it)
- Conversations (encrypted in private chats)

**Confidentiality Agreement:**
- Don't share others' personal information
- Don't record sessions without permission
- Don't share learning data publicly
- Don't mention others without permission
- Respect others' privacy settings
